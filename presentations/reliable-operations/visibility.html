PUBLICATION GOVERNANCE

Visibility is controlled and reversible

Every product keeps its store home.

Public exposure is a separate, auditable decision. It can be granted by a verified package entitlement or opened marketplace-wide by the administrator.

Store visibility is permanent · Public visibility is governed
01

Store only

Safe default in paid-publication mode. The seller store remains active and discoverable.

02

Products package

Verified payment grants the product a time-bounded place on the public Products page.

03

Home package

Verified payment grants Home exposure while the product remains in its seller store.

04

Home + Products

One entitlement can grant both destinations; expiry or revocation removes public exposure.

Free Public Catalog ModeWhen the administrator enables it, active products can appear on Home and Products without seller payment. When disabled, verified paid entitlements apply again.
