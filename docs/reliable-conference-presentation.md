# Reliable Premium Marketplace

## Cover
Reliable Premium Marketplace  
A trusted digital marketplace for customers, sellers, and communities  
Company and platform overview · August 2026

## Slide 1
Reliable turns independent stores into one trusted marketplace

- Customers discover products, stores, and services in one place.
- Sellers keep their own storefront while gaining marketplace reach.
- Administrators protect quality, payments, visibility, and trust.

## Slide 2
The platform connects three operating layers

- Customer layer: discover, compare, purchase, communicate, receive.
- Seller layer: create a store, publish products, fulfil orders, receive payouts.
- Admin layer: govern the marketplace, manage risk, and support growth.

## Slide 3
Discovery starts with Home, Products, and Stores

- Home creates confidence and introduces selected marketplace experiences.
- Products supports catalogue search, categories, and product-level shopping.
- Stores helps customers find independent sellers and browse complete collections.

## Slide 4
Every seller product has a permanent store home

- The seller storefront is the durable source for the product catalogue.
- Store-only visibility is the safe default in paid-publication mode.
- Search helps customers find active products and then discover the associated store.

## Slide 5
Customers move from discovery to delivery with clarity

- Product details explain price, stock, variants, delivery, and returns.
- Cart and checkout turn intent into an order.
- Order status, delivery confirmation, support, and returns complete the journey.

## Slide 6
Sellers get tools to operate, not just a listing page

- Store settings, products, variants, stock, delivery rules, and order handling.
- Product chat connects the seller with customers around a specific item.
- Payout profile and wallet controls support disciplined settlement.

## Slide 7
Visibility is controlled, measurable, and reversible

- Administrators configure Home, Products, and Home + Products packages.
- Verified payment activates a time-bounded entitlement.
- Free Public Catalog Mode can open the catalogue without seller payment.
- Expiry and revocation remove public exposure without deleting the store product.

## Slide 8
Trust is built into payment and payout operations

- Payment verification happens server-side against Paystack.
- Seller payout is separated from customer payment and settlement timing.
- Wallet accumulation protects sellers when the transfer threshold is not met.
- Idempotency prevents duplicate transfers and duplicate activations.

## Slide 9
Product chat feels familiar and accountable

- WhatsApp-style green message bubbles and chronological history.
- Sent, delivered, read, sending, and offline/retry states.
- Unread badges on product chat buttons help participants notice activity.
- Chat is for product communication, never for passwords or payment secrets.

## Slide 10
Administration is the marketplace control centre

- Registered sellers, stores, products, orders, payouts, reports, and settings.
- Security alerts and role-gated actions reduce operational risk.
- Branding, policies, visibility packages, and free catalogue mode stay under admin control.
- Every high-risk financial action requires verification and an audit trail.

## Slide 11
Reliable is designed for responsible growth

- More stores expand selection without losing seller ownership.
- More customers benefit from a consistent marketplace experience.
- Publication controls create optional commercial paths without forcing exposure fees.
- Operational separation keeps discovery, fulfilment, settlement, and support understandable.

## Slide 12
Reliable: discover with confidence, operate with discipline

- A marketplace built for customers, sellers, and communities.
- A clear operating system for trust, visibility, delivery, and payout.
- The next chapter is stronger participation with stronger controls.
