ADMINISTRATION AND GOVERNANCE

Administration is the control centre

The admin workspace turns marketplace complexity into visible decisions: who can operate, what customers can discover, how money moves, and how the system is explained.

Governance principle: administrators configure the rules, review the evidence, and intervene when the marketplace needs protection.
01

Sellers and stores

Review registered sellers, stores, verification, and business settings.

02

Products and orders

Manage catalogue quality, order status, reviews, and fulfilment visibility.

03

Payouts and finance

Inspect queues, thresholds, wallet accumulation, and transfer readiness.

04

Visibility and catalog

Configure packages, free public catalog mode, expiry, and publication rules.

05

Security and support

Monitor sign-in protection, communication, alerts, and operational issues.

06

Reports and operations

Use analytics, exports, delivery settings, promotions, and campaign controls.

07

Branding, manual, and presentations

Maintain the operating handbook, conference materials, logo placement, and institutional knowledge.

Reliable administration: control the system without obscuring how the system works.
