TRUST AND FINANCIAL DISCIPLINE

Trust is built into payments and payouts

Reliable keeps customer payment, Paystack settlement, seller wallet accumulation, and transfer authorization as separate, auditable operations.

01

Verify on the server

Paystack references, amounts, currency, ownership, and recipient details are checked outside the browser.

02

Respect settlement

Revenue shown in a dashboard is not automatically available for transfer; available balance and fees still matter.

03

Accumulate safely

Below-threshold seller earnings remain in the wallet ledger instead of triggering a premature payout.

04

Keep operations idempotent

Repeated confirmations do not duplicate entitlements, payout records, or Paystack transfers.

Operating principle: no transfer is safe until amount, fee, balance, recipient, authorization, and audit state agree.
